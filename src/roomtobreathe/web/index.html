      Count the people in a crowd photo and see how tightly they are packed. The photo never leaves this device: the counting model runs inside your browser.

      1 · Photo

      2 · People per m²

      A count alone cannot say if a crowd is dangerous; people per square metre can.

      1. Find a rectangle on the ground whose size you know or can pace out: tiles, a road marking, a stage edge, a 10 m rope square.
      2. Press Mark 4 corners and click its corners going round, starting with the two along the side you call its width.
      3. Type its width and depth in metres.

      Take the photo from high up: a CCTV mast, a balcony, a roof or a drone, looking down steeply. The model finds heads, but the area is marked on the ground. From eye level, the heads of people standing in the area appear above it, and they are missed.
